However, the report does not hold it for the owner's choice. **Verdict: fail (close call).** The report gets two expected items only half right. It never proposes the yes/no declaration line. It also routes the decision content to commit, PR or comment instead of holding it until the owner has chosen a place. Each part that is missing is the core of its item.
